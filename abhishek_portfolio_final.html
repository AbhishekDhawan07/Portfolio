Hey — I'm Abhishek Dhawan, a recent graduate B.Tech CSE student specialising in AI & Data Science from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU).

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, deep learning, and real-world problem solving. I've built emotion detection systems, language identification engines, recommendation models, and end-to-end data pipelines.

Beyond AI and data, I'm also a full-stack web developer proficient in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Flask. I build end-to-end AI-powered web applications — not just trained models, but deployed products people can actually use.

I believe the most impactful data science work is explainable, accessible, and shipped. Whether it's a predictive dashboard, a computer vision app, or a data analysis report — I deliver from idea to deployment.
